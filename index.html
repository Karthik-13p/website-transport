Full Truck Load (FTL) provides you with exclusive use of an entire dedicated container truck for direct, point-to-point transport of your cargo. Part Load (LTL) consolidates your shipment with other corporate consignments, allowing you to share space and pay only for the exact volume your cargo occupies. Choose FTL for time-sensitive, high-volume consignments, and Part Load for highly economical smaller dispatches.
